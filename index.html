About GideON

Revolutionizing EV Charging Infrastructure

We unify fragmented EV charging networks into a seamless, all-in-one platform for individuals, fleets, and businesses. Our app allows users to locate, book, and pay for charging effortlessly, with real-time availability and transparent pricing at their fingertips. With OCPI & OCPP integrations, we ensure direct connectivity to charge point operators, delivering maximum reliability and a hassle-free experience. From smart charging solutions to data-driven insights, we empower a smarter, more efficient, and future-ready EV ecosystem.

Why GideON?
Any Charger network, only One App
No more switching between multiple apps to find and use chargers. GideON integrates all major EV charging networks into a single platform for easy access.
Seamless Network Integration
We bring together all OEMs and charging providers under one umbrella. Users can charge at any station without worrying about compatibility issues.
Unified Wallet
Instead of keeping money locked in separate apps, GideON provides one wallet for all transactions. Users can track, manage, and spend their balance efficiently across all networks.
Multiple Payment Options
GideON ensures flexible, secure payments with no limits. Users pay seamlessly via UPI, cards, or wallets—no forced top-ups or restrictions.
Smart Search & Live Availability
Real-time charger status across OEMs helps users locate available stations seamlessly. Intelligent filters across OEMS allow users to search by distance, charger type, and price.
Hassle-Free charger bookings
Users can pre-book charging slots to avoid waiting in line. We can switch to nearby charger across OEMS in case of issues. Booking reminders ensure users never miss a slot.

Partnership Benefits

Why Players will Partner With Us

Maximize Charger Utilization & Revenue

  • More Footfall, More Charging Sessions – GideON's aggregated platform funnels more users to your chargers, ensuring higher utilization.
  • Optimized Pricing with Market Insights – While we never share one provider's data with another, our platform helps you understand overall demand trends, allowing you to adjust pricing smartly.
  • Higher charger occupancy, more revenue per station, and smarter pricing strategies.

Reduce Customer Acquisition Costs

  • Instant Access to a Large EV User Base – Instead of spending heavily on marketing your standalone app, your chargers are automatically visible to thousands of EV drivers on GideON.
  • Increased Brand Exposure – Users discover your charging stations through our unified platform, meaning you gain new customers without additional marketing costs.
  • Lower customer acquisition costs and increased bookings without extra ad spend.

Enhance Customer Experience & Retention

  • Seamless Charger Discovery & Booking – Users can easily find, book, and pay for your chargers without app-hopping, increasing the likelihood of repeat visits.
  • Data-Backed Service Improvements – Without sharing individual network data, GideON provides general industry trends, helping you refine charger availability, session times, and user experience.
  • Happier customers, more repeat users, and improved charger efficiency.

Solve the Wallet Fragmentation Problem

  • One Wallet for All Charging Networks – Users no longer have to maintain separate wallets, making payments across networks seamless and convenient.
  • Faster Transactions = More Sessions – A hassle-free payment experience means users are more likely to pick chargers available on GideON over standalone networks.
  • More completed charging sessions and a frictionless payment experience that keeps customers coming back.

Gain Data-Driven Insights

  • Intelligent Demand Prediction – GideON provides macro-level charging trends to help partners identify high-demand locations and peak usage times.
  • Smarter Expansion Planning – Without exposing competitor data, you get industry-wide insights to strategically place new chargers where they'll be most profitable.
  • Higher ROI on new charger installations and smarter network expansion.

OEM Integration Benefits

  • One Charging Solution for All Networks – GideON enables your customers to access and pay for any charger without worrying about network compatibility.
  • Embedded Charging for a Better Experience – Integrate real-time charger availability, navigation, and payments into your vehicle's infotainment system.
  • A superior EV ownership experience for your customers, increased brand differentiation, and a competitive edge in the growing EV market.
